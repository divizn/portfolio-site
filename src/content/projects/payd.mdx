A payments service with a JSON API to authorise, capture, refund and void card payments,
backed by PostgreSQL alone. Every write takes a Stripe-style idempotency key, every money
movement is a balanced pair of ledger entries, and every state change writes an outbox
event, all committed in one transaction.

## Guarantees

- One key, one effect: 50 identical concurrent creates made exactly one payment.
- No over-refund under concurrency: 50 refunds of 30 against a capture of 100 let exactly 3
  through.
- Crashes leave nothing half applied: 600 `SIGKILL`s under load left the ledger balanced
  with no outbox event lost.

## Performance

Throughput rose 51% (870 to 1,313 req/s) from sizing the connection pool for an acquirer
that takes 20 ms to answer.

## Testing

The tests were checked by reintroducing 11 bugs on purpose, such as dropping the unique
index on idempotency keys or writing the ledger in a separate transaction. 7 were caught on
all 20 runs. Authorisation goes to a fake acquirer that can be made slow, flaky or silent.
